Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we receive the name, phone number, email address, project type, location and message you enter. We collect nothing else through the form.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your construction enquiry and to prepare a quotation. We do not sell your details or share them with third parties for marketing.

Processors

Enquiries are delivered through Formspree (formspree.io), which processes the form on our behalf. This site also uses Vercel Web Analytics, which measures page views without cookies or personal profiles.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and our business records. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your details at any time.
