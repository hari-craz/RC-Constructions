# Claims review (`docs/CLAIMS-REVIEW.md`)

**This is an editorial risk reduction, not legal advice.** It replaces absolute and guarantee-style wording with factual, process-based statements, and removes facts that nobody supplied. The company should have its final wording reviewed by its own adviser before launch.

A test (`src/content/claims.test.ts`, plus `e2e/phase-13.spec.ts` on the rendered page and the project overlay) fails if any of these appear in visible copy: zero, never, always, every, guarantee, 100%, fully, ensure, no defects, certified, accredited, licensed/licence, registered, award. The list and the (currently empty) allowlist are in `src/content/claims.ts`. The legal pages are written separately and are reviewed by the adviser.

Licences, accreditations, registrations, test results, standards compliance and safety records are not stated anywhere unless the company supplies them in `src/content/company.ts`.
