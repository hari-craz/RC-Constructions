# Design review, Phase 14D (`docs/DESIGN-REVIEW-phase-14d.md`)

Method: a fresh subagent with no access to code or earlier versions saw only contact sheets of the frames in `docs/review/phase-14d/after/` (house at six angles on desktop and phone, the morph at twelve scroll values at 1440x900, 390x844 and 768x1024, every section and stage at 390x844, 768x1024, 1904x1080 and 360x640, and the layout either side of 1024 px) and the goals. Its findings and my responses are below. This file records findings and what is still weak; it does not declare the work finished. `before/` holds the Phase 14C frames.

## What changed

- **Detail**: about 1,000 vertices, 470 faces, 21 solids, four groups (massing, openings, surfaces, porch and ground), flat fills in three tones. Frame cost at a 4x CPU slowdown: median 3.4 to 3.5 ms (peaks to 4.6 ms) against a 4 ms target (Phase 14C house: about 1.8 ms). See `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`.
- **Morph**: a pure function of the scroll (`src/lib/morph.ts`): front elevation, landing on the building's ground line and footprint (`siteTarget`, `landing`), top-first dissolve, plinth left behind, foundation begins where it stood.
- **Below 1024 px**: a fixed full-screen background (house, then building, centred in the screen) behind transparent sheets; muted drawing; Work delivered on plates; no band, no slide-over.
- Owner request after the first review pass: the phone building is centred in the screen (it stood in the lower part before).

## Weakest points still on the page

1. Text over the building on phones and tablets is legible by measurement but busy: lit or muted-amber windows and structure lines run behind Services and Projects text.
2. On phones the house is wider than the screen for a short stretch of the morph (10 to 20%), and the dissolve itself is dim because the dark house is a muted ghost.
3. Side and rolled views of the house have a few ambiguous roof and porch relationships; the underside of roofs has no detail.
4. No JavaScript shows only the static house; no finished building follows it.
5. The phone control strip (eight buttons) still crowds the top of the hero.
6. Frame cost sits at the 4 ms target with peaks above it at a 4x slowdown.
