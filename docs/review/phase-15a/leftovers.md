# The leftover list for the Phase 15A review

These are the leftovers the release-candidate phase was asked to close (from the "still weakest" lists of earlier reviews). The reviewer judges only from the handover screenshots and the demo script.

1. Text over the building on phones looks busy. (Hairlines and detail of the background building run behind the text; the gap between text blocks is narrow.)
2. The phone house dissolve is dim. (The house should be at full tone until the dissolve starts, and dimmed only during the dissolve, only below 1024 px.)
3. The phone control strip is crowded. (On phones: Turn, Tilt, Reset and Auto-rotate only; roll behind a "More" disclosure and on a two-finger twist; targets at least 44 px, no wrapping at 360 px.)
4. Without JavaScript, the page should show the static house in the hero and the finished building after it.
5. Side and rolled views of the house have ambiguous roof and porch relationships.
6. Earlier layout items: a grey band above About, wide-screen balance on Founder and Contact, a clipped Services heading on phones.
7. Sample content must be marked as sample on the page, never presented as real (contact details, projects).
